Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Game progress on your device

VG RISE stores gameplay data, including scores, climbed floors, hearts, and other game progress, only on your device. The app does not upload or sync this gameplay data. You do not need an account to play. Removing the app may clear your local progress.

Ads and install attribution

  • Google AdMob serves in-app ads. Depending on SDK settings, device permissions, and region, Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related operations. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • AppsFlyer helps measure installs and where they came from. Its SDK may process installation and launch events, device and app information, and identifiers where available and permitted. See AppsFlyer’s Services Privacy Policy.

Tracking choices

You can allow or deny tracking if iOS displays the App Tracking Transparency prompt. To change your choice later, go to Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Changing this setting does not necessarily remove ads or stop processing needed to deliver the app. See Apple’s tracking settings guide.

Support email and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you include. We use them to respond and troubleshoot. These pages do not use advertising scripts, web analytics scripts, or cookies of their own. The hosting provider may keep standard access logs.

Sharing, retention, and requests

Information may be processed by the service providers described above to operate the app and website or disclosed when required by law. We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes described here, taking into account legal requirements and the retention practices of our service providers.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or object to or restrict certain uses. Email lengmo714@gmail.com to make a request. We may ask for information needed to locate your data and verify the request.

Children and changes

VG RISE is a general audience game. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, email lengmo714@gmail.com so we can review the situation. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
