Get help with VG RISE for iOS.

Contact support

Email lengmo714@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, VG RISE version, what happened, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help. Please avoid sending passwords or payment information.

How do I play?

Your tile moves from side to side. Tap the screen to jump to the next platform. In Classic mode, you can choose your jump timing. In Lightning mode, the screen keeps scrolling, so you need to keep climbing.

What happens when I run out of hearts?

Choose Refill hearts to request an interstitial ad and add five hearts. If you refill on the results screen, you can then choose Play again to start a new run in the same mode.

What if the app is stuck or my progress is missing?

Close and reopen the app, then confirm you have a stable connection if it is checking for updates or loading an ad. Scores are saved on your device. Removing the app can clear that progress, so contact us before reinstalling if you want help troubleshooting a missing score.
