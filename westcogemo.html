WeChat-Style Conversation Generator for Mockups and Demo Screenshots

Classify at: Tools
Words: 137 Read:≈ 1min

Try the tool

This small tool can generate WeChat-style conversation screenshots for mockups, demonstrations, or harmless jokes.

Appearance settings: It includes controls for details such as the mobile signal, network status, device time, charging state, battery level, unread-message count, and chat background.

Conversation settings: You can select participants, assign avatars, add red-packet or transfer entries, and set voice-message durations. Notes for red packets and transfers can also be customized. To remove an entry, place the pointer over it and click the red ×, or clear the entire conversation.

The generated screenshots can look highly realistic, which is also a useful reminder that chat screenshots found online should not automatically be treated as proof.

Use generated screenshots only for lawful mockups, education, or entertainment. Do not use them to impersonate someone, fabricate evidence, deceive others, or cause harm.
