Technical Support

Need help using VG Poker Equity? Start here or email us directly.

How to calculate

  • Choose Texas Hold'em, Omaha, or Short Deck.
  • Tap a card slot, choose a suit, then choose a rank. Complete both players' hole cards.
  • Optionally add the flop, turn, and river in order. Tap Start to calculate.
  • Change cards and tap Start again to update the result. Use Clear to reset the matchup.

Why can't I select a card?

A card can only be used once in a matchup. In Short Deck, ranks 2 through 5 are unavailable. Add all three flop cards before selecting the turn, and add the turn before selecting the river.

Why do preflop results change?

Preflop equity uses random simulations, so small differences between runs are expected. Later streets use the remaining possible outcomes.
