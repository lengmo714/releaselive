Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

VG Poker Equity is a poker analysis app. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the app simple.

Information the app handles

The cards you select are used on your device to calculate equity and possible outs. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or location. We do not receive your selected cards or calculation results through the app.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive the information you include in your message and your email address. We use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Children

The app is an analysis tool and does not offer real-money gambling. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. The latest version will be posted here with an updated effective date.
